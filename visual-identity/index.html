01 / motion

Arena & storm

This is the real game renderer in a preview frame: worn slate and runes, a precise safe-circle boundary, violet storm mist, and exposure warnings from real gameplay.

Live specimen · current game renderer

Storm edge

Layered violet cloud banks, wind streaks and local lightning flashes cover unsafe ground. The pale blue boundary follows the exact safe-circle radius; Vikings remain visible through the foreground mist.

Battle effects

Axe swings, recoil, blood and fallen bodies respond to combat. Exposed Vikings show a filling ring and time remaining before storm elimination.

Arena texture

Uneven slate slabs with shaded edges, sparse moss, worn runes and a central compass carving fade into the purple page. The cached ground stays fixed as the safe circle shrinks.
